A linear mixed model was fitted for Time with |a| \textgreater{} 2.5 m/s2 (\%).
The effect of \textit{time} on Time with |a| \textgreater{} 2.5 m/s2 (\%) was significant ($b = 0.32$, 95\% CI $[0.04, 0.60]$, $t(314) = 2.26$, \p{0.025}).
The effect of \textit{level} on Time with |a| \textgreater{} 2.5 m/s2 (\%) was not significant ($b = -5.23$, 95\% CI $[-10.63, 0.16]$, $t(314) = -1.91$, \p{0.057}).
The effect of \textit{slope} on Time with |a| \textgreater{} 2.5 m/s2 (\%) was not significant ($b = 0.56$, 95\% CI $[-0.93, 2.06]$, $t(314) = 0.74$, \p{0.459}).
