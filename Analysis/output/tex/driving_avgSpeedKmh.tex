A linear mixed model was fitted for Mean speed (km/h).
The effect of \textit{time} on Mean speed (km/h) was significant ($b = 0.59$, 95\% CI $[0.31, 0.86]$, $t(314) = 4.26$, \pminor{0.001}).
The effect of \textit{level} on Mean speed (km/h) was significant ($b = -7.13$, 95\% CI $[-12.37, -1.88]$, $t(314) = -2.67$, \p{0.008}).
The effect of \textit{slope} on Mean speed (km/h) was not significant ($b = -1.06$, 95\% CI $[-2.51, 0.40]$, $t(314) = -1.42$, \p{0.155}).
