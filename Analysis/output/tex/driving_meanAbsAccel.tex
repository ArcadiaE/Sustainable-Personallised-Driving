A linear mixed model was fitted for Mean absolute acceleration (m/s2).
The effect of \textit{time} on Mean absolute acceleration (m/s2) was significant ($b = 0.02$, 95\% CI $[0.00, 0.04]$, $t(314) = 2.48$, \p{0.014}).
The effect of \textit{level} on Mean absolute acceleration (m/s2) was not significant ($b = -0.21$, 95\% CI $[-0.53, 0.12]$, $t(314) = -1.26$, \p{0.209}).
The effect of \textit{slope} on Mean absolute acceleration (m/s2) was not significant ($b = 0.00$, 95\% CI $[-0.09, 0.09]$, $t(314) = 0.10$, \p{0.924}).
