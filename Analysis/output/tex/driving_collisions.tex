A generalized linear mixed model was fitted for collision count.
The effect of \textit{time} on collision count was significant ($IRR = 1.05$, 95\% CI $[1.03, 1.07]$, $z = 5.51$, \pminor{0.001}).
The effect of \textit{level} on collision count was significant ($IRR = 0.72$, 95\% CI $[0.53, 0.98]$, $z = -2.08$, \p{0.038}).
The effect of \textit{slope} on collision count was not significant ($IRR = 0.97$, 95\% CI $[0.90, 1.06]$, $z = -0.62$, \p{0.533}).
